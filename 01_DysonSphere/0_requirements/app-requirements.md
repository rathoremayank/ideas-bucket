# Personal Development Tracking App

Goal: build a web app to track all major areas of personal growth in one place, so I can monitor progress, stay consistent, and make better decisions about how I spend my time and energy.

## Core idea
A personal dashboard for life improvement across multiple dimensions of growth, such as:
- health and fitness
- career and skills
- finances
- relationships
- learning and knowledge
- mindset and emotional well-being
- habits and routines
- creativity and hobbies
- environment and lifestyle

## App vision
The app should help me answer questions like:
- What am I working on right now?
- Which areas of my life need attention?
- Am I improving over time or just staying busy?
- What habits are helping me grow?
- Where am I inconsistent or overwhelmed?

## Main modules

### 1. Life areas dashboard
Create customizable categories for personal development, for example:
- Physical health
- Mental health
- Career growth
- Learning
- Relationships
- Spirituality / values
- Productivity
- Finances
- Creativity
- Lifestyle / home

Each area should have:
- a score or progress indicator
- goals and milestones
- current habits
- notes and reflections
- recent activity

### 2. Habit tracker
Track daily or weekly habits such as:
- exercise
- reading
- journaling
- meditation
- deep work
- sleep schedule
- hydration
- no-phone evenings

Features:
- mark complete/incomplete
- streak tracking
- weekly summary
- habit health score
- reminder notifications

### 3. Goal tracker
Set goals with:
- title
- category
- target date
- reason/purpose
- milestones
- current status
- priority level

Examples:
- Run 5K in 3 months
- Read 12 books this year
- Build a portfolio project
- Save $5,000
- Sleep by 11 PM for 30 days

### 4. Journal and reflection
Daily or weekly reflection space to capture:
- wins
- lessons learned
- mood/emotions
- blockers
- gratitude
- action items

This can help connect patterns between behavior and outcomes.

### 5. Progress analytics
Show trends over time:
- weekly score by life area
- monthly growth summary
- habit consistency chart
- goal completion rate
- best and worst performing areas
- momentum trends

This helps turn personal development into something measurable.

### 6. Focus planner
A weekly plan view to schedule:
- top 3 priorities
- time blocks for learning, exercise, recovery, deep work
- energy level tracking
- tasks tied to personal goals

### 7. Personal scorecard
A summary screen that evaluates each area on a simple scale, such as 1-10, based on:
- habits
- consistency
- progress toward goals
- self-reflection quality

This gives a quick overview of where the user is thriving and where they need attention.

## Key user experience ideas
- Simple and clean dashboard
- Mobile-friendly layout
- Minimal friction for daily check-ins
- Motivating visual progress (streaks, charts, badges)
- Ability to customize categories and metrics
- Private personal space with no social pressure
- Light and dark mode

## Suggested pages
- Dashboard
- Life areas overview
- Goals page
- Habit tracker
- Journal
- Weekly planner
- Analytics
- Settings

## Example features for version 1
- Add/edit personal life areas
- Create habits and track completion
- Set and update goals
- Log daily journal entries
- View simple dashboard cards for progress
- Weekly summary email or in-app report
- Basic analytics charts

## Example features for version 2
- AI-powered insights on patterns and weak areas
- Recommendation engine for habit suggestions
- Mood and energy correlation analysis
- Goal dependency mapping
- Progress export / PDF reports
- Integrations with calendar, fitness trackers, or notes apps

## Product principles
- Track consistency, not perfection
- Measure progress over time
- Keep the experience motivating and non-judgmental
- Make personal growth actionable, not abstract
- Support both short-term habits and long-term transformation

## Final concept
A web app that acts like a personal development operating system: one place to define what matters, track consistent actions, reflect on growth, and see progress across every sphere of life.
